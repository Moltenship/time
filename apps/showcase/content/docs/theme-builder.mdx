Pick a preset or change a token, and the calendar follows. Light and dark have their own values:
the preview starts in the docs' theme, and **Light** and **Dark** above it switch the theme you see
and edit. Your changes stay in the preview, and nothing is saved.

## Paste the CSS into your app

The CSS has the shape of `calendar-theme.css`: tokens inside `@layer base`, light values on
`:root` and dark ones on `.dark`. Paste it into your global CSS after the line that imports
`calendar-theme.css`, so it comes later in the same layer and wins. A shading recipe comes after the
tokens, outside the layer, as on [Availability](/docs/availability#customize-the-shading).

- **Changed tokens** copies what differs from the theme as it ships. A token you changed only in
  light mode is restated in `.dark` too, so the dark theme keeps its own value.
- **All tokens** copies the whole theme, to replace the variables in your copy of the file.

Rules your app writes outside a layer win over the pasted ones: its own shadcn theme, or overrides
like those in [Changing tokens](/docs/theming#changing-tokens). Change those tokens there instead.

## What you can change

- **Colors.** The tokens in [Theming](/docs/theming#tokens), and the shadcn tokens under **Base
  colors**. Many calendar tokens derive from those: grid lines are `var(--border)`, and the default
  event fill mixes `--primary` into `--background`. A derived token follows its source until you
  set it. `--calendar-surface` isn't here: it's unset, and you set it on a container, as Theming
  shows.
- **Density.** The hour height and the event padding in time grids, and `--radius`, which rounds
  events, buttons and badges.
- **Availability.** The tints for closed time and closed days, a shading recipe for closed time in
  time grids, the busy and free dots, and the outlines of changes the policies reject or flag. The
  recipes are the ones on Availability, but the tint stays the **Closed time** token's.
- **Event palette.** Each color's hue and chroma, and how every color derives its fill, text and
  edge from them, per theme. To add a color, see [Adding a color](/docs/theming#adding-a-color).

**Contrast** checks, as the preview paints them, the pairs the theme's own test holds to WCAG AA on
the background, plus body text and the hour labels: 4.5:1 for text, 3:1 for the now line, the
focus ring and the status marks. When event text falls short, it names the palette color with the
lowest contrast.

Menus, tooltips and popovers open outside the preview, in the docs' own theme. A light preview on
a dark page also keeps a few dark-mode touches of the shadcn controls, such as the **Today**
button's fill, since the page's `dark` class reaches them.
