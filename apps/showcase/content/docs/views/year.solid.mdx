Try it in the [Solid demo](https://moltenship-time-demo.vercel.app/solid/?view=year): it opens on
the year.

`createYearView` shows the anchor's calendar year, January to December, as twelve small month
grids. Weeks start on `weekRules.firstDay`. Each date shows once: a week that reaches into the
month before or after leaves those cells empty, so every month keeps its own shape.

## Add it

Add the `year-view` item to your app with the shadcn CLI, as
[UI source](/docs/installation#ui-source-optional) describes. It brings the theme, the shared
`calendar` parts and the kit's popover:

```bash
npx shadcn@latest add @moltenship/year-view
```

Then register the view, with the month its month names open. A date opens the view `dayView`
names, `day` by default; this calendar has no view by that name, so it opens the month:

<include meta='title="calendar.tsx"'>../../examples/solid/year-page.tsx#view</include>

The view is named `year`. Previous and Next move by one year. Its range is the whole year, so
`onVisibleRangeChange` asks for a year of events at a time. Where the months don't fit, the view
scrolls inside the height it's given, so give `Calendar.Viewport` a height:

<include meta='title="year-page.tsx"'>../../examples/solid/year-page.tsx#page</include>

## What it shows

- **Months:** two to a row on a phone, up to six on a wide screen. Each keeps room for six weeks, so
  months side by side line up.
- **Days:** today's date sits in a filled circle and is `aria-current="date"`. Under each date, a
  dot for every event color on that day, up to three, in the order the day's events start. All-day
  and multi-day events count on every date they cover.
- **A day's events:** clicking a day opens a popover with its events, all-day first, then by start
  time. Each event is a `<button data-event-id>`, so the app's own event details open from it as
  from any other view. The date at the top opens the day in the view `dayView` names, `day` by default. Register a
  view by that name, or point `dayView` at one you have, so the click has somewhere to go. The popover
  follows the day's events as they change, and a new year closes it.
- **Month names** open that month in the view named `month`.
- **Closed days:** with [availability](/docs/availability), see [below](#closed-days).
- **Scrolling:** where the months don't fit, the view opens with today's month, or the day you last
  focused, in view. Today, Previous, Next and a picked date scroll their month into view. Only the
  view scrolls, never the page around it.
- **Targets:** every day is 24 px across, so a finger finds it on a phone.
- **Right to left** mirrors the months and their weeks when the page or `Calendar.Frame` has
  `dir="rtl"`.
- **Reduced motion** opens the popover without its scale and slide.
- **Editing:** none. The year shows events without moving or creating them; open a day or a month
  to edit.

## Closed days

With [availability](/docs/availability), a day without working hours is named "closed" for screen readers,
as in the month view. What's shaded depends on `closedDays`:

- `"exceptions"` (the default) shades the dates an exception closes, such as a public holiday or an
  offsite. Weekly closed days, such as weekends, stay plain: the weekday columns already show them,
  and a third of the year in gray would bury the holidays.
- `"all"` shades the weekly closed days too.
- `"none"` shades nothing.

Shaded days next to each other in a week join into one band with rounded ends, so a weekend or a
long holiday reads as one stretch, and the rows stay apart. To draw it differently, such as
hatching, pass `renderUnavailable`; its `band` carries the reason, the exception's label and
whether open time meets each end.

## Anatomy

`YearView` renders the parts below. The view builds them itself from its projection, so they're not
parts you compose: to change one, edit its file under `components/calendar/year-view`.

```txt title="Rendered by YearView"
YearView                         year-view.tsx
├── YearMonthGrid                year-month.tsx, one per month
│   ├── Month name               opens the month view
│   ├── Weekday initials
│   └── YearDayCell              a gridcell, data-date, one per day
│       ├── UnavailableTime      the shading of a closed day
│       └── PopoverTrigger       the date and its event dots, data-color, one per color
└── YearDayPopover               year-day-popover.tsx, one for the whole year
    ├── Date                     opens the day view
    └── YearEvent                a [data-event-id] button per event
```

The popover is one popover from the kit, with a trigger in every day that shares it through a
handle, so the year never mounts hundreds of popovers.

The `.tsx` files are Solid components. What they render comes from plain TypeScript beside them:

| File             | Holds                                                                     |
| ---------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| `year.styles.ts` | Every class the parts set, so a restyle is one file                       |
| `year-labels.ts` | The dates, start times, day names and the popover's words                 |
| `year-layout.ts` | The dots a day shows, which days are closed, and which month to scroll to |

## Keyboard

The [Accessibility](/docs/accessibility#keyboard) page has the full map.

| Focus | Key                | Effect                                                                   |
| ----- | ------------------ | ------------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| A day | ← →                | Previous or next day, across months                                      |
| A day | ↑ ↓                | Same weekday a week earlier or later, across months                      |
| A day | Home, End          | First or last day of the week's row in its month                         |
| A day | Page Up, Page Down | Same day in the previous or next month                                   |
| A day | Enter, Space       | Open the day's events. Tab moves through them; Escape returns to the day |
| A day | Shift+Enter        | Open the day in the day view                                             |

The year is one tab stop. Moving past December 31 or before January 1 navigates to the next or
previous year.

## Build your own

The strategy pieces are exported from `@moltenship/calendar-solid`, for a year that looks
different, such as a heat map of busy days: `getYearRange`, `navigateYear`, `getYearTitle`,
`projectYear` and `yearSpatialNavigation`. `projectYear` returns twelve months of week rows, each
day with its event fragments in display order, or `null` outside the month. See
[Custom views](/docs/views/custom-views).
