The `calendar` item has three parts that move a calendar around:

| Part              | File                   | What it does                                                                      |
| ----------------- | ---------------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| `CalendarToolbar` | `calendar-toolbar.tsx` | Today, previous and next period, and the title, with room for your own actions    |
| `ViewSwitcher`    | `view-switcher.tsx`    | A select of the views, with a keyboard shortcut beside each one in the list       |
| `MiniMonth`       | `mini-month.tsx`       | A month of dates that picks the calendar's date and shows the range the view does |

## Add them

Add the `calendar` item with the shadcn CLI, as
[UI source](/docs/installation#ui-source-optional) describes. A view such as `list-view` brings it
with it, and it brings the [controls](/docs/views/controls) it's built from:

```bash
npx shadcn@latest add @moltenship/calendar
```

## Use them

Render them inside `Calendar.Root`. The toolbar takes your calendar's parts, and the view switcher
and the mini month read the calendar's state with its hooks:

<include meta='title="calendar-page.tsx"'>../../examples/solid/calendar-page.tsx#toolbar</include>

`CalendarToolbar` reads `calendar` once, when it renders. Its children go at the end, after the
title: put the view switcher and your own actions there, and pass `start` for content before the
Today button, such as a sidebar toggle. Its buttons are the package's `TodayTrigger`,
`PreviousTrigger` and `NextTrigger`, so they step by the view's own period and are named for screen
readers.

`ViewSwitcher` doesn't know your views. Give it `options`, each a `label`, a `value` and an optional
`shortcut`, the `value` that's chosen, and `onValueChange`. The shortcut shows in the list only, and
the switcher binds no key to it.

`MiniMonth` doesn't read the calendar either, so it can sit outside `Calendar.Root`. It shows the
month of `selectedDate` and follows it when it changes, marks `today`, and draws `visibleRange` as
a band. `onSelectDate` gets the date a person picks.

## Keyboard

| Focus            | Key                | Effect                                           |
| ---------------- | ------------------ | ------------------------------------------------ |
| A mini month day | ← →                | Previous or next day                             |
| A mini month day | ↑ ↓                | The same day a week before or after              |
| A mini month day | Page Up, Page Down | The same day a month before or after             |
| A mini month day | Enter, Space       | Pick the day                                     |
| The view select  | ↓, ↑, Enter, Space | Open the list; then move through it and pick one |
| The view select  | A letter           | The next view whose label starts with it         |
| The view select  | Escape             | Close the list, and keep the view                |

The mini month is one tab stop, and moving past its first or last day shows the month before or
after. Its keys run before the calendar's, so it works inside `Calendar.Frame` too. Picking a view
from the list puts focus back on the select.

## Screen readers

The toolbar's Previous and Next buttons are named "Previous period" and "Next period", and their
tooltips, which open after 400 ms on hover and on keyboard focus, label them. The view select is a
`combobox` named "View", and its list a `listbox`. The trigger reads the view's label alone, without
its shortcut.

The mini month is a `grid` named by its heading, "October 2026", which is a live region, so a
screen reader hears the new month after a step. Each day is a button named by its full date,
"Thursday, October 8, 2026". Today's is `aria-current="date"`, and the selected day's cell is
`aria-selected`.

## Look

In a right-to-left page, the arrows point the other way and the buttons keep their places at the
inline start and end. With reduced motion, the select's list and the tooltips fade in without
growing. On a touch screen, every target is at least 24 px. The parts take their classes from
`calendar-toolbar.styles.ts` and `mini-month.styles.ts`. Edit them to restyle the parts.
[A control's look](/docs/theming#a-controls-look) lists every such file.

The words, such as "Today" and "Next month", are in `navigation-labels.ts`. Change them there to
translate the parts.
