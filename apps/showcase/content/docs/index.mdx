Moltenship Time handles the hard parts of a calendar: dates, time zones, navigation, calendar
state, fast event lookups, and saving changes. Your events stay in the shape your app already uses,
and Time reads them through a small adapter. The week, day, month, and agenda views are source files
you copy into your app, so you can change how they look and behave.

## Who it's for

Time is for React developers building scheduling screens (bookings, rooms, shifts, team calendars)
who want to:

- keep their existing event data and API,
- style the calendar with their own design system, or start from shadcn/ui,
- get time zones and daylight saving time right without writing that math themselves.

## What you get today

- **Built-in views.** [Week, day](/docs/views/week-and-day), [month](/docs/views/month), and
  [agenda](/docs/views/agenda), in the style of Google Calendar, built on shadcn/ui and Tailwind
  CSS. A [theme](/docs/theming) with calendar tokens and an event color palette comes with them.
- **Your data, read through an adapter.** You describe how to read an ID, title, and time range
  from your records. TypeScript keeps your record and ID types all the way through.
- **Three kinds of time.** Timed events use `Temporal.Instant`, all-day events use
  `Temporal.PlainDate`, and floating events use `Temporal.PlainDateTime`. No `Date` objects.
- **Time zone and DST handling.** Days that are 23 or 25 hours long and skipped or repeated hours
  are handled for you.
- **Editing.** Moves, resizes, creates, and deletes arrive as changes for you to save. They show
  as pending while they save, roll back if saving fails, and pass through policies you choose. See
  [Interactions](/docs/interactions). The built-in views support dragging, resizing, and creating
  events with the mouse, a pen, or touch.
- **Controlled or uncontrolled state.** The visible date, view, time zone, selection, and focus can
  each be owned by the calendar or by your app.
- **Headless parts.** `Root`, previous/next/today buttons, `Title`, and `ActiveView` render plain
  elements, and [custom views](/docs/views/custom-views) are a strategy plus a component.
- **Server rendering.** During server rendering, Time doesn't read the clock, the host time zone, or
  `window`, so the server and the browser render the same markup from the same props.
