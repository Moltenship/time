A calendar is made of the parts your `createCalendar` factory returns, such as `Calendar.Toolbar`,
and a few components from the registry. The diagram labels each part with its name in code, and the
code below it puts the same parts together.

<CalendarAnatomy />

```tsx
<Calendar.Root
  events={meetings}
  defaultAnchorDate={today}
  defaultTimeZone="Europe/Berlin"
  locale="en-US"
  weekRules={weekRules}
>
  <Calendar.Frame>
    <Calendar.Toolbar>
      <Calendar.TodayTrigger>Today</Calendar.TodayTrigger>
      <Calendar.PreviousTrigger aria-label="Previous period" />
      <Calendar.NextTrigger aria-label="Next period" />
      <Calendar.Title />
      <ViewSwitcher value={view} onValueChange={setView} options={viewOptions} />
    </Calendar.Toolbar>
    <aside>
      <MiniMonth
        locale="en-US"
        selectedDate={anchorDate}
        onSelectDate={setAnchorDate}
        today={today}
        weekStartsOn={1}
      />
    </aside>
    <Calendar.Viewport>
      <Calendar.ActiveView />
    </Calendar.Viewport>
  </Calendar.Frame>
</Calendar.Root>
```

- **`Root`** holds the calendar's state and renders no element of its own. Every part and hook
  works anywhere inside it.
- **`Frame`, `Toolbar`, `Title`, `Viewport`,** and the three triggers each render one element with a
  `data-calendar-part` attribute, such as `data-calendar-part="today-trigger"`, for styling. The
  [component reference](/docs/api/react#components) lists them.
- **`ActiveView`** renders the active view's Renderer inside the viewport.
- **`ViewSwitcher`** and **`MiniMonth`** come from `registry/calendar`. They're plain components:
  you connect them to the calendar with `setView`, `setAnchorDate`, and the `view` and
  `anchorDate` state. `Calendar.ViewSwitcher` is an unstyled `<select>` you can use instead.

The registry's `CalendarToolbar` renders the toolbar parts above, styled, and puts its children at
the end. The [Quick start](/docs/quick-start) uses it:

```tsx
<CalendarToolbar calendar={Calendar}>
  <ViewSwitcher value={view} onValueChange={setView} options={viewOptions} />
</CalendarToolbar>
```
